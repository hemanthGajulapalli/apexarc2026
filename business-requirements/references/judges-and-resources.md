## Reference Links

- **CodeKata** (Dave Thomas) — origin of the "kata" concept applied to
  software practice: http://codekata.com/kata/
- **Architectural Katas** (Ted Neward) — the parent format this event is
  based on: https://www.architecturalkatas.com/
- **The Katalog** — a library of example/reference architectures
  ("read the architecture"): https://github.com/thekatalog
- **Communication Patterns: A Guide for Developers and Architects**
  (Jacqui Read, O'Reilly) — cited for the "successful communication ...
  results in shared understanding" framing used in the brief:
  https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/communication-patterns/9781098140533/

## Quotes Worth Keeping in Mind

- "The goal of a diagram is to convey a clear and shared understanding of
  the architecture." — Neal Ford
- "Successful communication is the art and science of sharing or
  exchanging ideas and information, ... resulting in shared
  understanding." — Jacqui Read
